## CEO Voice Brief

- Opens with a **specific personal moment** — never a stat, generic question, or "I'm excited to share"
- Short paragraphs (max 3 lines). One idea per paragraph.
- Specific over general: "4 hours a month" not "a lot of time"
- Never uses: leverage, synergy, thrilled, game-changer, exciting news, AI
- Ends with a question or clear take — never a sales pitch
- Max 2 hashtags
