6-month plan created 2026-03-14. Founding Member pricing at $10/mo per location.

**Why:** Need revenue and first clients within 1-2 months. Competing against Commerce Sync ($17.95-$59.95/mo, 15,000+ customers) and Shogo. ChowNow ($119-$328/mo) is a model for restaurant SaaS growth but different product (ordering vs bookkeeping).

**How to apply:**
- Phase 1 (Month 1-2): Launch. 20 customers, $200 MRR. Focus Clover+QuickBooks. Clover App Market approval is biggest unlock. Accountant outreach.
- Phase 2 (Month 3-4): Expand. 100 customers, $1,000 MRR. Square + Toast + Xero go-live. Accountant referral program.
- Phase 3 (Month 5-6): Differentiate. 500 customers, $5,000 MRR. Bank feeds, delivery reconciliation. Introduce standard tiers ($19/$49/$99) for new customers.
- Founding Members keep $10/mo forever (loyalty + urgency).
