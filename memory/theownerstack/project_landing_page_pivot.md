SalesToBooks (formerly AutoBooks/TheOwnerStack) pivoted to a full restaurant financial platform.

**Why:** The vision is to become the ChowNow of restaurant bookkeeping — an all-in-one financial autopilot for restaurant owners. Current pricing is Founding Member at $10/mo per location (locked forever for early adopters). Future tiers: Sync $19/mo, Agent $49/mo, Autopilot $99/mo (after 500+ customers).

**How to apply:** Landing page follows ChowNow's pattern: profit-focused messaging, integration grid, demo-driven CTA. No fake social proof — demonstrate potential through integrations (18+ active/coming soon) and AI features. Integrations page at /integrations shows 6 categories: POS (Clover, Square, Toast, Lightspeed), Accounting (QB, Xero, FreshBooks), Delivery (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub), Banking (Plaid), Payroll (Gusto), AI & Insights.
